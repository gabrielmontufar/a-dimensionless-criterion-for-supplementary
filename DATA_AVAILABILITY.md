# Data Availability

FoRDy and FoRCy mastersheets used for the experimental proxy-class validation, the FoRDy mastersheet used for the OpenSeesPy 3D solver-backed class-prediction layer, and the FLIQ spreadsheet tables used for the liquefaction-oriented false-safe audit, are redistributed in `data/raw_redistributed/` with hashes recorded in `DATA_SOURCES.csv` and `MANIFEST_SHA256.csv`.

The main manuscript conclusions are reproducible offline from these included files. Historical field-data audits, including Garner Valley and optional controlled-dynamic transfer checks, are retained only as bounded falsification or secondary context and are not required for R0 or R1.

The package does not claim full nonlinear 3D response-history validation or independent prediction of force histories.
